# 2026-09-17 — Kawagoe municipal disposal cost-floor opportunity

## Finding

For cleanup-cost intent, the strongest trust/conversion gap is not another vendor list. It is a concrete **municipal cost floor vs paid full-service cleanup vs as-is property sale** comparison.

Kawagoe City updated its household-waste item search on 2026-09-15. The current official bulky-waste route is reservation-based and paid; the city's bulky-waste page states collection can be up to one month out and excludes TVs, air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines/clothes dryers and PCs from municipal collection. The official household-waste manual also points residents to self-delivery facilities.

## SERP implication

A fresh 2026-08-28 competitor page for Yokosuka already uses the useful pattern `自治体処分の費用感 → 業者依頼 → 片付けずに売る`. A 2026-09-11 article also targets `残置物 + 不動産買取 + 処分費用 + 現状渡し`. This confirms the intent cluster is active, but generic articles are getting crowded.

Do **not** create another generic `実家片付け費用` article or municipality-page batch. Improve the existing Kawagoe high-intent cleanup-cost page first.

## Recommended module for existing high-intent page

Working heading: `片付け費用を払う前に、3つの手残りを比較`

Compare:
1. **自分で市の制度を使う** — cheapest cash-cost route where practical, but requires sorting, carrying/attendance and may take time.
2. **片付け業者に必要範囲だけ依頼** — useful when distance, volume or physical work makes DIY unrealistic; compare identical scope and lawful household-waste transport arrangements.
3. **家財を残したまま売却・処分案を比較** — for sale-intent owners, value the property before full clearance and compare net proceeds/time burden against separate clearance.

Never state that one route is always cheapest overall. Compare cash cost, time, travel/attendance, sale price/net proceeds, and family burden.

## Monetization rule

This module should strengthen—not replace—the primary monetization path. Sale-intent visitors should still reach the empty-home/disposition comparison CTA before paying for full clearance. TownLife Empty Home Solution remains the first live-test offer once the issued tracking URL is available. The logged-in ASP dashboard remains authoritative for payout/approval terms.
