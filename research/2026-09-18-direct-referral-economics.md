# Direct referral economics — 2026-09-18

## Decision
Prioritize validation of a direct referral partnership for inherited vacant-home sale leads before adding more SEO pages.

## Newly verified candidate
idea株式会社 announced a multi-industry partner program on 2026-08-04 covering Tokyo/Kanagawa/Chiba/Saitama.

Published conditions:
- registration / annual fee: 0 yen
- payment: only when the referred property sale closes
- information fee: 90,000–350,000 yen per closed referral, depending on transaction band
- eligible sources include estate-clearance / household-goods / demolition / professional-services businesses; applicability to a media/SEO site must be confirmed before implementation
- customer consent is required for referral
- entry does not itself mean a partnership agreement

## Why this matters
The currently documented affiliate route pays roughly 10,000 yen per approved lead. A compliant direct-referral route could materially change revenue per qualified visitor, so partner eligibility and tracking should be validated before investing in more informational content.

## Funnel implication
Keep the editorial promise neutral: decide the exit before paying to clear everything. For users who explicitly want to sell, test a high-intent referral path only after partnership approval. Do not present this as an active offer until eligibility, agreement, consent flow, attribution, payment conditions, and privacy handling are confirmed.

## SERP observation
Current vacant-home competitors increasingly lead with free valuation, inherited-property sale, and acceptance of remaining household goods. This reinforces the existing strategy: high-intent exit pages over thin municipality-page expansion.
