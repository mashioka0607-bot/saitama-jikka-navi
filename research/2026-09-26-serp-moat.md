# 2026-09-26 SERP / monetization review

## What changed

A current SERP competitor (anshin-jikkajimai.com) is now using almost the same surface-level pitch as this project: "片付け不要・残置物そのまま", comparing cleanup cost with sale proceeds, and publishing city-specific pages such as Saitama City. Other current competitors also emphasize "そのまま買取", including homes with furniture left behind.

This means `片付けずに売る` alone is no longer a sufficient moat. Do **not** respond by mass-producing city pages.

## Decision

Strengthen the site around a **pre-contract audit / net-proceeds decision** rather than generic local landing pages:

1. Before paying for cleanup, obtain an as-is valuation.
2. Compare net proceeds after cleanup, repairs, demolition, holding costs, taxes and brokerage costs.
3. Before demolition, check rebuildability / road access, loss of residential-land tax treatment, subsidy timing and asbestos obligations.
4. Preserve public/non-commercial exits (municipal vacant-home bank / consultation) when they are economically better for the user.
5. Monetize only where user intent has progressed to valuation / disposal; keep informational public-service routes neutral.

## Primary-source quality note

Saitama Prefecture states (page updated 2026-09-03) that before demolition, renovation or repair, asbestos-containing building materials must be investigated for **all materials in the relevant work area regardless of project size**. This is a useful differentiator for the pre-demolition checklist and should be cited from the prefectural source rather than paraphrased from contractors.

Source: https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/b0506/k-taisui/271/isiwata.html

Saitama Prefecture also describes municipal vacant-home banks as a matching route between owners who want to sell/rent and people who want to buy/rent; retain this as a neutral exit option instead of forcing every visitor into a paid lead.

Source: https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/a1107/akiyabanku.html

## Measurement gate

Do not expand this cluster into additional pages until Search Console can show query x landing-page impressions/clicks and GA4 can attribute CTA clicks. Current repository config still has blank `ga4_id`, blank `affiliate_url`, and an empty `offers` array. Once measurement is live, compare the above clusters by revenue per 1,000 organic sessions, not ranking alone.
