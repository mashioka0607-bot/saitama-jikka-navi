# 2026-10-02 SERP / monetization check

## Decision change
Add a **registration readiness / deadline** branch ahead of cleanup and sale. This is more defensible than another generic cleanup article because it is a prerequisite to a clean sale and is backed by primary government information.

## Fresh primary-source signal
- Inheritance registration has been mandatory since 2024-04-01: generally within 3 years after learning of acquisition by inheritance.
- For inheritances predating the mandate, the transitional deadline is 2027-03-31.
- Address/name change registration became mandatory in 2026-04: generally within 2 years after the change.

This means the diagnostic should not stop at “whose name is the property in?” It should also ask whether inheritance registration is complete and whether the registered owner address/name is current.

## SERP / commercial-intent signal
Current difficult-property competitors continue to monetize direct purchase around inherited vacant homes, shared interests, remaining contents and rebuilding restrictions. The stronger opportunity for this site is to catch the user one step earlier: **can this property be sold cleanly now, and what must be fixed before requesting quotes?**

## Saitama primary information reconfirmed
Saitama Prefecture's page updated 2026-09-28 says there are about 130,000 vacant homes without a utilization purpose, about 1.8x the level 20 years ago. Detailed statistics published 2026-09-04 report 330,000 vacant homes total and 136,000 without a utilization purpose (3.8%). Municipal vacant-home banks remain an official sell/rent exit, and the prefectural existing-home distribution network remains active.
