# 2026-09-26 SERP decision — generic four-exit comparison is becoming commoditized

## Fresh finding

A fresh SERP check for `実家 売る 貸す どっち 空き家 比較` found a strong national-brand result from MISAWA RELAY (published 2026-08-02) explicitly comparing the same four generic exits: **sell / rent-use / demolish / keep-managing**. Another current result (`親の家これから相談室`) compares sell / rent / keep for the parent-entering-care-home scenario.

## Decision change

Do **not** build a generic standalone “売る・貸す・持つ・解体の4択比較” page/tool as the next feature. That framing is no longer a moat and would mostly reproduce an already crowded SERP pattern.

Instead, strengthen the existing defensible layer: **pre-contract / pre-irreversible-action audit + total net outcome**. The site should answer the questions competitors often leave unresolved immediately before money is spent:

1. Is ownership / inheritance status ready enough for the next action?
2. Before clearing contents, what is the property worth with contents remaining?
3. Does contents-included purchase beat clear-then-broker on total net proceeds and time?
4. If renting is plausible, what repair/setup/vacancy/management burden changes the result?
5. Before demolition, are subsidy application timing, asbestos survey, rebuildability, tax effects and sale-value impact checked?
6. What does waiting 1–3 years cost in carrying costs and risk?
7. Which exit leaves the highest **net** amount after all relevant costs, not the highest headline price?

## SEO / GSC tests

Prioritize modifiers that signal an unresolved economic decision rather than generic “what are my options” intent:

- `片付けてから売る どっち`
- `空き家 現況買取 仲介 比較`
- `実家 解体して売る そのまま売る`
- `実家 売るか貸すか 費用`
- `空き家 売却 維持費 手残り`
- `実家 家財 そのまま 売却`

If impressions appear, improve `/tedori-hikaku/` and `/kaitai-check/` before creating another comparison article. Judge by revenue / 1,000 organic sessions and qualified CTA rate, not pageviews alone.

## Primary-source context

Saitama Prefecture's page updated 2026-09-04 reports about 330,000 vacant homes statewide, including about 136,000 with no utilization purpose; the latter increased by about 12,000 versus the prior survey. The prefecture also maintains municipal vacant-home banks for both owners who want to **sell or rent**, so a neutral exit comparison remains appropriate, but it should be used as supporting trust/context rather than as a generic SEO product.

## Monetization implication

No payout/ASP-condition change was verified in this run. Keep the current rule: commercial valuation/referral CTA only where sale/disposition intent is strong; official/neutral routes remain visible for undecided users. Do not insert guessed tracking URLs.
