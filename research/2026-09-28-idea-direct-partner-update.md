# 2026-09-28 monetization update — idea direct partner terms materially improve the opportunity

## Verified primary-source finding

idea株式会社's current official `空き家・相続不動産 連携パートナー制度` page (checked 2026-09-28) explicitly includes Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba and **Saitama** and says the information fee schedule was **revised in August 2026**.

Current published fee schedule (tax included):

| Sale price | Brokerage closes | idea buys directly |
|---|---:|---:|
| <= JPY 10m | JPY 220,000 | JPY 330,000 |
| > JPY 10m and <= JPY 30m | JPY 330,000 | JPY 330,000 |
| > JPY 30m | JPY 440,000 | JPY 330,000 |

Payment is success-based: fee arises only after idea actually receives its brokerage fee in a brokerage case, or after idea completes purchase settlement in a direct-purchase case. Payment is by the end of the following month. No fee if written customer consent is missing, idea/another route already had the lead, or the sale contract occurs more than 12 months after referral. Refund can apply if brokerage compensation is returned or the direct-purchase contract is cancelled/rescinded/voided.

The program is a B2B information-referral arrangement, not a normal click affiliate. The partner may hear the problem, ask whether the owner has sale intent, obtain the owner's permission and connect the owner to idea. idea handles valuation, brokerage agreement, important-matter explanation, sale contract, settlement and handover. The partner must not negotiate price or perform brokerage acts.

## Mandatory disclosure / UX constraint

idea publishes mandatory disclosure wording. A partner must clearly tell the customer that the partner will receive an information fee if a sale contract is reached, that only idea is being introduced, and that the customer may choose another real-estate company. Website advertising/referral copy requires idea's prescribed wording and prior approval.

Therefore **do not put a live idea referral CTA/form on the site until the business is accepted, the written agreement is signed, idea approves the site wording/placement, and the consent/privacy handoff is implemented**.
